Engineering guardrails

Phone audio is affected by automatic gain control, noise suppression, microphone placement and frequency response. This prototype does not provide calibrated SPL, gear-mesh order, pinion RPM, or a definitive diagnosis.

Initial target: compare dominant tones between controlled runs. Next development step: save WAV audio and snapshots, then add run-to-run overlays. Order tracking requires a reliable speed reference; microphone-only speed estimation is not assumed.
